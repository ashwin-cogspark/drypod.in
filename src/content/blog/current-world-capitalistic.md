I think the world is becoming increasingly capitalist. And everyone, including myself, is becoming opportunistic.

I'm welcoming the kind of problem-solving that can be done by throwing money. But whichever problem demands more than what I have, I become offended and blame the 'capitalistic' world.

Everyone wants to be in the first bucket. They want to be rich enough that throwing money at their problems wouldn't hurt them financially. 

In a world where everyone is rich, inflation will be so high that the current Indonesian currency would look like a joke (no offence intended). 

The clique of the rich knows this already (though it is not any secret, anyone able to think independently would come to the same conclusion). 

So, it is my belief that they are lobbying in whichever way they can, to maintain the divide between rich people and poor people, which in turn sustains their world. 

See ya!
