I caught up with the manhua 'The Book Eating Magician'. There were 114 chapters translated after which they announced hiatus. It's been roughly two years since then but not a chapter has been released. 

Being left in an incomplete way, I started searching for the manhua's web novel continuation. Then I found out that the web novel was fully translated and licensed on Wuxiaworld. So, I've decided to start reading the web novel from where the manhua left. 

See ya!
