I've started reading *The Prince* by Machiavelli today. Though I have yet to complete the first chapter, I have read the preface-like parts of the book.

Machiavelli is described as a person whose talent shone in literary endeavours alone. He was timid-natured when it came to his personal business but strict and did not mince words in his literary works. 

Even though in this book he only wrote the reality between the ruler and the ruled, he was misunderstood as evil. The accounts mentioned in this book are rooted in his experiences as a bureaucrat in the Office of Florence (Sry, I don't remember his exact position).
