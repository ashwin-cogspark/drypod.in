I caught up with the latest translated chapter of 'A Mountain of Corpses, A Sea of Blood' manhua. I like the story so far. Different from a normal reincarnation where only the MC reincarnates, here his entire team reincarnates (save for one person).

So, onto the next one, 'The Book Eating Magician'. It is about a Magic Academy student who cannot perform magic. He encounters an ancient being, Gluttony, who can grant him magic power provided he satiates its hunger with magic books.

With the thought of getting back to 3D printing, I downloaded a model and started the print job. It is a multi-laptop stand. I needed this as my two work laptops were taking up space on my table.

With the same purpose in mind, I went ahead and ordered four different colours of PLA Filament from Amazon. Luckily there was a sale on-going.
