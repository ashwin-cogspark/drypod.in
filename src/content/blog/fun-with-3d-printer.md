I had so much fun with my A1 Mini yesterday. A specs holder stand, a couple of cute cat figurines for my mom and sis, a few sets of clips for food packs, and a monitor shelf - these are the items I printed last weekend.

The filament roll that I had bought when I got the 3D printer is over. Luckily, I had booked four more filament rolls since the Amazon Independence Day fest was going on. One of those arrived just in time for me to continue the prints without hiccups.

My father is not so happy with my purchase of these filament rolls. I can understand him to an extent. From his perspective, these are a waste of money. At my age people are getting married and getting on with their life whereas I'm just printing stuff that can be considered toys.

Ever since I can remember, none in my family had any serious hobby. So, all we spent was for needs or wants that can be considered extensions or upgrades of needs, but never for sheer pleasure. Now here I am spending Rs.800 on average for each filament roll which is pure splurge.

I understand his perspective but I don't think I can make him understand my perspective. So, I just need to be sure to limit my further purchase of filament for a while (or get the filament delivered when no one is at home and silently dispose of the package 😉). 

See ya!
