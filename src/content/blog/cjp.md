CJP is more an influencer movement for virality than a meaningful political movement, yet.

I get this impression when looking at the news and reading the newspaper that the protest was a result of an attempt to provide some kind of altruistic meaning to a satirical group which was started to spite the Chief Justice of India.

The irony is that it was a meme which began to mock CJI's comments on the youth but it actually had the opposite effect and proved CJI's comments were correct.

Even the leader himself didn't expect the meme he started to gather as much support as it did. Realising that he was about to become the meme of his own satirical movement, which at this point of time was a social media handle and a website, he needed to divert attention elsewhere.

The NEET paper leak, which happened just a few days back, became the diversion. NEET always had its problems -- few people against it and few people for it. Tamil Nadu being vehemently against NEET (I'm yet to form my opinion on this).

With the NEET paper leak, CJP found a goal, which was to demand Education Minister Pradhan's resignation. I fail to understand how this will solve any of the problems with NEET.

After nearly two months of protest, Education Minister Pradhan resigns and CJP celebrates victory (as if the Education Minister was the one behind the paper leak).

All this while, anyone and everyone began marketing and reacting on social media. People traveling to Jantar Mantar, people at Jantar Mantar -- everyone continued posting for clout and followers. This is where the meaning for the movement gets muddied. Did these people really come wishing for a change or did they come because of FOMO -- the virality associated with the protest? I'm leaning towards the latter.

One thing I can agree on is that it became a 'protestival' where everyone participating in the protest began celebrating being a participant.

A Bill also passed which increased the punishment for those involved in such leaks. This seemed like a consolation prize; taking an action without taking an action. Just like how my sister twists and turns in bed for an hour before waking up while still convincing me and others that she was already awake from the beginning.

Now what happened to CJP? Where are they?

See ya!
