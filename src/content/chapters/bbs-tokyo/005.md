Chapter 5 — Group Seven

Keio University's Hiyoshi Campus was a different planet.

Gao Qiang walked through the west gate at 2:40 PM, twenty minutes early, and felt the shift immediately. Manicured lawns. Students in designer casual — Canada Goose jackets, Supreme backpacks, the subtle flex of Aesop hand cream pulled from Balenciaga totes. The air smelled like money and privilege and the particular confidence of people who had never been hungry.

His three shirts were clean. That was the best he could do.

The library was a glass-and-concrete monument to academic ambition. He found Group 7's reserved study room on the third floor and opened the door.

Two people were already inside.

The first was a lanky Japanese guy with bleach-blond hair and a skateboard propped against the wall. He was scrolling through his phone with the boneless sprawl of someone who was here because attendance was mandatory, not because he intended to contribute. His name tag read **OGAWA RYO**.

The second was Park Soo-jin.

She was exactly what her text message had suggested: efficient, sharp-edged, and radiating the specific impatience of someone who had already sized up the room and found it wanting. Korean, twenty-one, hair cut in a precise bob that framed a face designed for magazine covers and a mouth designed for giving orders. She was arranging documents on the table with the speed and precision of a card dealer.

"You're early," she said without looking up. "That's acceptable."

"High praise."

"Don't get used to it." She slid a folder across the table. "Yamada's assignment is a strategic analysis of a real-world Japanese company — financial health, market position, growth projections. We have four days. Ogawa hasn't read the brief. Have you, Ogawa?"

The blonde kid didn't look up from his phone. "skimmed it."

"You skimmed it." Park Soo-jin's voice could have frozen ramen broth. "Incredible. We're counting on you."

"I'm good for the presentation part," Ogawa said. "I can talk. You guys do the numbers."

Park Soo-jin closed her eyes for a moment. The kind of closing that said *I am imagining violence and it is helping.*

Gao Qiang sat down and opened the folder. The target company was **Tanaka Heavy Industries**.

His blood went cold.

Tanaka Heavy Industries. Where Kenji had worked. Where Kenji had been destroyed. The company connected to Kuroda-gumi's money laundering operation.

He kept his face neutral. "This is an interesting pick."

"Yamada assigned it specifically," Park Soo-jin said. "Said it's a complex case — diversifying conglomerate, some recent restructuring. The kind of thing that separates the serious students from the, well." She glanced at Ogawa. "Skimmers."

"Hey," Ogawa said, mildly offended.

Gao Qiang didn't hear him. He was reading. The brief included publicly available financials — balance sheets, income statements, cash flow reports for the last three fiscal years. To the untrained eye, Tanaka Heavy Industries looked healthy. Modest growth, stable margins, nothing alarming.

But Gao Qiang had been reading balance sheets since he was fourteen. And his eyes snagged on something in the cash flow statement within thirty seconds: a line item labeled "Consulting Services — Overseas Partnerships" that had grown from ¥800M to ¥2.3B in two years with no corresponding revenue increase on the income statement. Money going out. Nothing coming back in.

*That's where the laundering happens,* he thought. *Consulting fees to shell companies. Classic.*

"I'll take the financial analysis," he said.

Park Soo-jin raised an eyebrow. "You sure? It's the hardest section."

"I'm sure."

She studied him for a moment — the worn shirt, the tired eyes, the absolute steadiness of his hands on the documents. Something shifted in her assessment. Not respect, exactly. Recalibration.

"Fine. Ogawa, you're on industry overview and competitor benchmarking. I'll handle strategic recommendations. Gao Qiang — I need your section by Thursday noon so I can integrate."

"Done."

"Good." She gathered her papers with crisp efficiency. "Meeting adjourned. Don't be late next time."

She was out the door before either of them could respond. Ogawa finally looked up from his phone.

"She's terrifying," he said admiringly.

"She's efficient," Gao Qiang corrected. "There's a difference."

"Nah, man. She's terrifying. But like — hot-terrifying, you know?" Ogawa grinned. "The kind where you're scared but you're also kinda into it."

Gao Qiang didn't dignify that with a response. He lifted his phone and started photographing the financial documents — a habit he'd developed not for studying, but because every piece of information about Tanaka Heavy Industries was now ammunition.

He left the library with his mind running on two tracks. Track one: the group project, which was legitimate academic work and also a goldmine of intelligence on his enemies. Track two: the system, which had been quiet since last night but was now pinging with a new notification.

---

**⚠ HIDDEN SUB-MISSION DETECTED: 「 The Name on the Ledger 」**
*Your academic research has uncovered information connected to Revenge Target #1. Follow the trail.*
*Reward: Identity confirmation of Revenge Target #1, 150 LP*
*Failure: Trail goes cold for 30 days*

---

A hidden sub-mission. Tied to the group project. Gao Qiang felt something tighten behind his sternum.

Zhang Wei. He was close.

---

That evening, the apartment was no longer empty.

Kenji had used 15,000 yen of the household fund — Gao Qiang noticed he'd stopped thinking of it as *his* money — to buy secondhand furniture from a recycling shop. A low table for the living room. Two futons. A small refrigerator that hummed like a sleeping animal. A kettle. Four bowls, four cups, four sets of chopsticks.

"Four?" Gao Qiang asked.

"We'll have a fourth soon," Kenji said, as if this were self-evident.

Tanaka Mei arrived at 8 PM, still in her waitress uniform, carrying a plastic bag from her restaurant. Two containers of leftover ramen — not the menu kind, the staff kind, made from whatever was left at closing.

"I brought dinner," she said, setting it on the low table. Then she saw the furniture. The futons. The bowls. Her mouth opened, closed.

"Welcome home," Kenji said.

Something cracked in Tanaka Mei's expression. Just for a second. Then she squared her shoulders and sat down at the table with the precise movements of someone who wasn't going to cry, absolutely not, not in front of anyone.

They ate. The ramen was mediocre — too much salt, not enough broth — and it was the best meal any of them had had in weeks.

Then the system chimed.

---

**✦ MISSION COMPLETE: 「 First Night 」✓**

*72-hour evaluation window expired. Housing maintained. Brotherhood: 2 members active.*

**Rewards:**
- 200 LP (Total LP: 220)
- Blueprint: Safe House (Level 1) — Upgrades to apartment now available
- **NEW MISSION CHAIN UNLOCKED: 「 The Iron Throne 」**

---

A second notification followed immediately:

---

**「 The Iron Throne 」 — Mission 1: Intelligence Network**

*Deploy 2+ Brothers/Sisters on information-gathering operations simultaneously.*
*Reward: 100 LP, Ability Upgrade for deployed members*

---

Gao Qiang let out a breath he hadn't known he was holding. Three days of uncertainty, dissolved in a single chime. Two hundred LP. The breathing room he needed.

He caught Kenji's eye. Kenji had felt it too — the shift in the air when the system pulsed. He didn't understand it fully, but he understood the result. His Big Brother had delivered.

After dinner, Gao Qiang laid out the situation. The Golden Goose. Nakamura. The leverage. The group project and Tanaka Heavy Industries. The hidden sub-mission pointing toward Zhang Wei.

And he laid out his next move: deploy two people on intelligence at the same time.

"Mei," he said. "I need you to use Street Whisper on The Golden Goose and the three blocks around it. Staff movements, customer patterns, delivery schedules — anything that tells us how that bar operates and who comes and goes."

Mei nodded. "My shift ends at 10 tomorrow. I can cover the area from 10:30 to 2 AM."

"Kenji — you're back at The Golden Goose tomorrow night. Same approach. Be indispensable. And keep your ears open for any mention of the name Zhang Wei. Or any Chinese national connected to Tanaka Heavy Industries' consulting partnerships."

Kenji's eyes sharpened. "You think the laundering and Zhang Wei are connected?"

"I think Tanaka Heavy Industries is paying two billion yen a year to 'overseas consulting partners' and not getting anything in return. I think Zhang Wei fled China with 200 million yuan and needed a place to wash it. I think the connection is right there in the public financials, and no one's looked because no one thought to look."

Silence. The refrigerator hummed.

"That's why you're at Keio," Kenji said slowly. "Not despite the scholarship. Because of it. Access to databases. Research infrastructure. Corporate records."

"The best place to hide a tree is a forest," Gao Qiang said. "The best place to find one is the same."

He opened the system and confirmed both deployments. The interface glowed.

---

**✦ MISSION UPDATE: 「 Intelligence Network 」 ✦**

**Deployment 1:** Murakami Kenji → The Golden Goose (Infiltration/Financial)
**Deployment 2:** Tanaka Mei → Kabukicho Block 7-9 (Surveillance/Informant)

**Status:** 2/2 deployed. Mission activated.

**ETA for first intelligence report:** 24-48 hours.

**Passive Effect:** Deployed Brothers gain +10% experience toward next level while on active assignment.

---

"Two operations running," Gao Qiang said. "We move carefully. We gather information. And we wait for the right moment."

"And what do you do, Big Brother?" Tanaka Mei asked.

Gao Qiang picked up his phone and opened the camera roll to the photos he'd taken of Tanaka Heavy Industries' financial statements.

"I do my homework."
