Chapter 2 — The Kabukicho Rulebook

Gao Qiang didn't sleep that night. Not because of the booth, which he'd grown used to, and not because of the system, which was still sending him tutorial notifications every twenty minutes like an overeager smartphone game.

He didn't sleep because he was thinking.

The system had given him a framework, but frameworks without strategy were just cages. His father had taught him that. *Qiang'er, a business plan isn't what you're going to do. It's understanding what everyone else is going to do and positioning yourself before they do it.*

He pulled a notebook from his suitcase — the one with the broken wheel — and started writing.

**ASSETS:**
- 340,000 yen (personal)
- 50 LP (system)
- Murakami Kenji — Manager Class, Level 1. Former senior accountant. Blacklisted but highly skilled.
- Big Brother Aura (passive) — ???

**LIABILITIES:**
- No housing
- No legitimate income
- No legal residence status complications (student visa intact, but dorm loss needs resolution)
- Unknown enemies (Zhang Wei, others?)
- No network in Tokyo

**IMMEDIATE GOALS:**
1. Complete Mission: Secure housing (72 hours)
2. Generate income
3. Recruit more brothers — but strategically
4. Maintain cover as student

He tapped the pen against his lips. The system said brothers generated income, but Kenji was a Manager, not a Fighter. He wasn't going to be shaking down anyone in an alley. His value was in his brain — and in the system's listed ability, **Paper Trail**.

*Kenji can identify financial irregularities in any business within 24 hours of access.*

Gao Qiang's eyes narrowed.

In Kabukicho — the neon-lit entertainment district he'd been calling home — every other building was a cash business. Bars, clubs, massage parlors, pachinko dens, love hotels. Cash businesses meant two sets of books. Two sets of books meant financial irregularities. Financial irregularities meant leverage.

He wasn't going to build an empire with muscle. Not yet. He was going to build it with *information*.

---

Morning. The Net Paradise 24 was quiet at 6 AM, populated only by a few overnighters who hadn't yet shuffled out. Gao Qiang found Kenji in the drink bar area, nursing a green tea and scribbling in the margins of yesterday's notebook — the asset list Gao Qiang had given him, already annotated with Kenji's own observations.

"Big Brother," Kenji said, rising. The title came naturally to him now. Not forced. Not ironic. Like he'd been waiting his whole life to say it to someone who deserved it.

"Sit. Let's talk."

Gao Qiang laid out his thinking. The notebook. The asset list. The plan.

Kenji listened with the focused attention of a man who had spent decades in boardrooms reading between the lines of financial statements. When Gao Qiang finished, Kenji was quiet for a long moment.

"You're thinking about leverage," Kenji said.

"I'm thinking about *where* to get leverage. You said Tanaka Heavy Industries fired you for exposing fraud."

"Not just Tanaka." Kenji's jaw tightened. "The fraud was a cover for money laundering. Tanaka is connected to the Kuroda-gumi — one of the last traditional yakuza families in Tokyo. They own businesses throughout Shinjuku. When I filed the whistleblower report, I didn't know I was stepping on Kuroda's toes. By the time I figured it out, it was too late. They didn't kill me — that would have been too messy. They just... destroyed everything else."

Gao Qiang filed the name away. **Kuroda-gumi.** Another coal for the fire in his chest.

"So the businesses in Kabukicho — the bars, the clubs —"

"Many of them pay tribute to Kuroda," Kenji said. "Or to smaller gangs that pay tribute to Kuroda in turn. It's a pyramid. He sits at the top."

"And if someone had financial information on those businesses — tax irregularities, unreported income, licensing violations —"

"That someone would have leverage over the entire food chain." Kenji's eyes sharpened. The old accountant was surfacing, the one who had once terrified junior staff with his ability to find a single mismatched entry in a ten-thousand-line ledger. "But getting access to their books would require —"

"Getting hired. Or getting someone on the inside." Gao Qiang tapped the notebook. "That's where more brothers come in. But first, we need a base of operations."

He looked at the system's mission timer: **62 hours remaining.**

"Show me cheap apartments in Shinjuku," Gao Qiang said. "One that won't ask too many questions about a Chinese student and a fifty-two-year-old Japanese man moving in together."

Kenji almost smiled again. "I know a place. A real estate office on Yasukuni-dori. The owner owes... well. He owes everyone. He'll be flexible."

---

The apartment was on the fourth floor of a building with no elevator and a ground-floor yakitori restaurant that vented its smoke directly into the stairwell. It was a 2LDK — two bedrooms, a living-dining area, and a kitchen the size of a closet. The wallpaper was yellowed, the tatami in the second bedroom was stained, and the bathroom had the distinct aesthetic of 1987.

It was 90,000 yen per month. No key money. No guarantor fee. Move-in today.

Gao Qiang paid three months upfront — 270,000 yen — because the system's **Big Brother Aura** passive ability, which he was only beginning to understand, seemed to make landlords just... agreeable. The real estate agent, a nervous man with sweat stains on his shirt, had started the negotiation at 120,000 with a 200,000 key money deposit and ended it at 90,000 flat with a look of mild confusion, as if he couldn't quite remember why he'd ever asked for more.

**Remaining funds:** 70,000 yen.

It was enough. It had to be.

Kenji carried the suitcase up the four flights — insisted on it, actually, with a stubbornness that surprised Gao Qiang. "You're the Big Brother," Kenji said, as if that explained everything. Maybe it did.

They stood in the empty living room. No furniture. No curtains. Just sunlight coming through dirty windows and the distant sound of Kabukicho already waking up below.

"First order of business," Gao Qiang said. "We need to get you into a position where you can use Paper Trail. That means getting you access to business financial records."

Kenji nodded slowly. "There's a bar three blocks from here. **The Golden Goose**. It's a Kuroda-tied establishment — one of the smaller ones. They've been running without a proper accountant for months. The owner is a man named Nakamura. Violent temper, not much brains."

"You think you can get hired?"

"I think," Kenji said, and his voice carried a weight that Gao Qiang hadn't heard before — the weight of a man who had nothing left to lose, "that a desperate, blacklisted accountant walking in and offering to organize their books for below-market rates is exactly the kind of opportunity a man like Nakamura has been waiting for."

"Good. Do it tomorrow. Take whatever they offer."

"And you, Big Brother?"

Gao Qiang looked out the window. Kabukicho stretched below them — a labyrinth of neon and shadow, money and misery, opportunities and traps. Somewhere in this city, Zhang Wei was counting stolen yuan. Somewhere, Kuroda was sitting on his pyramid. Somewhere, the next brother was waiting to be recruited.

"Me?" Gao Qiang said. "I have classes to attend. And a system to figure out."

He opened the system interface and scrolled past the tutorial notifications to a section he hadn't explored yet:

---

**BIG BROTHER SYSTEM — LORE DATABASE**

**Entry 001: The Origin of Brothers**

*Every Brother recruited by the System is chosen based on a single criterion: their life was broken by forces beyond their control, and they chose to endure rather than surrender. The System does not create loyalty. It reveals what was already there — the unbreakable bond between those who have nothing left to lose and the one who gave them a reason to stand.*

*The Big Brother does not command through fear. He commands through investment. Every resource spent on a Brother is a seed. Every seed grows into an army.*

*But beware: the Iron Rule exists for a reason. Brothers who hoard. Brothers who profit for themselves. These are not Brothers — they are parasites. The System will not protect them.*

*And neither will the Big Brother.*

---

Gao Qiang closed the interface. Below the window, Kabukicho hummed with its ten million nightly transactions — money changing hands, secrets being sold, lives being ruined and rebuilt in the same breath.

He was going to take all of it. Not with guns. Not with fists. With the oldest weapon in business: *information, and the will to use it.*

But first — a knock at the door.

Gao Qiang and Kenji exchanged glances. No one knew they were here. They'd literally just moved in.

Gao Qiang crossed the empty living room and opened the door.

A woman stood in the hallway. Mid-twenties. Black hair pulled back in a ponytail. Sharp eyes behind wire-framed glasses. She was wearing a cheap blazer over a white blouse and carrying a canvas tote bag with a police academy logo faded on the side.

"Good morning," she said. Her voice was flat, professional, and carrying the particular weariness of someone who worked nights. "I'm Sakamoto Yui, community liaison officer with the Shinjuku Police Station. We do rounds for new residents in the area. May I ask a few questions?"

Her eyes flicked past Gao Qiang to Kenji, then back. Taking in the Chinese face. The Japanese older man. The empty apartment. The lack of furniture.

Processing.

Gao Qiang smiled. It was the smile his father had taught him — warm, open, completely concealing every thought behind it.

"Of course, Officer. Please, come in. Though I should warn you, we don't have any chairs yet."

Sakamoto Yui's expression didn't change. She stepped inside anyway.

And somewhere in the system interface, a new notification quietly appeared:

---

**⚠ ALERT: Attention Detected**

**Sakamoto Yui** has been flagged by the System.
**Occupation:** Police Officer, Shinjuku Station (Community Liaison)
**Threat Level:** Moderate
**Recruitment Viability:** Unknown — Requires further assessment
**Note:** This individual is not random. She has been assigned to monitor activity in this building following reports of Kuroda-gumi interest.

*The Big Brother System advises: Watch carefully. Every threat is an unrecruited Brother in disguise.*
