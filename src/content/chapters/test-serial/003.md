The fourteenth floor at 1:00 a.m. had a specific silence, the kind with a hum in it. Air handlers, server room, the drinks machine downstairs cycling its compressor. Forty desks, thirty-eight dark. One lamp burned in the far corner where Nakamura from receivables kept the hours of a man avoiding something at home, and one burned at Kenji's station, where the Fujimoto forecast was entering its ninth draft.

Fujimoto Logistics, Thursday, new anxiety: the numbers had finally stopped being fine. A tariff schedule nobody had modeled was going to bite the third quarter, and the third quarter was going to bite Kenji, and the only variable he controlled was the phrasing.

He was, at that moment, 6% into Foundation Establishment, and stuck.

The stagnation rules had clarified themselves over three days of careful field notes, which he kept in a password-protected file named FY26_Q3_PORT, because no one in the history of Marukoshi Trading had ever opened a file with a name like that. Rule one: qi withheld in proportion to mental absence. Rule two: the ledger forgave nothing and forgot nothing. Rule three, discovered Wednesday in a budget meeting that ran ninety minutes over: an emotion fully *felt* — not suppressed, not fed, just felt, the way you hold a live wire before deciding to let go — posted as a deposit.

Sawatari's face at the two-hour mark of that meeting had been worth 22 qi. Kenji had felt his own fury arrive, named it mine, and watched it clear through the ledger like sediment through a filter.

But tonight there was nothing to feel. Just the forecast, and the hum, and 6% — a number that had been 6% since Tuesday. He had run the arithmetic a hundred times on the ride to stagnation and back: eleven hundred qi per day, if he rode resonantly, sat presently, and felt honestly. Foundation required ten thousand. He was a salaryman with a quota, and for the first time in eleven years, the quota was inside his own body.

He tried the cycle. Four in, six out, tongue to palate. The office after midnight had a resonance of its own, he'd found — the building's deep sixty-cycle hum — and riding it earned 4 qi a breath, same as the Yotsuya curve.

【STAGNATION DETECTED. REASON: FATIGUE, 3%. MINOR.】

"Noted," Kenji said, to no one, and kept breathing.

At 1:20 a.m., Nakamura left without a word, the way 2 a.m. men do, and Kenji was alone on the floor with the hum. At 1:34, the lights in the northeast quadrant clicked off, motion sensors giving up on him, and the darkness came across the ceiling like a tide until it reached the edge of his lamp.

And in that specific dark — forty desks, one lamp, one hum — the warmth under his sternum, which had spent three days merely turning, turned over.

【CAUTION: SPONTANEOUS BREAKTHROUGH IN PROGRESS.
THE PRACTITIONER IS ADVISED TO REMAIN SEATED.
ADDITIONAL ADVICE: THIS WILL FEEL LIKE A PERFORMANCE REVIEW.】

It did not feel like a performance review. It felt like the Chuo line at rush hour, inside his spine. Something that had no business moving stood up in the space between his navel and his breastbone and demanded right of way, and every meridian it found was a single-lane street with parked cars. Kenji gripped the armrests of a nine-thousand-yen office chair and did the only thing he knew: he kept the ratio. Four in. Six out. The cycle was the desk. The cycle was the desk. Whatever arrived, arrived to a man at his desk, breathing.

The pressure crested, stopped being pain, became — briefly, horribly — *interest*, the way a knot becomes interesting five minutes before it yields, and then something in his chest gave, not like breaking, like a filing cabinet unlocking.

【FOUNDATION ESTABLISHMENT: 7%.
MERIDIAN: GOVERNING CHANNEL, PARTIAL. QI CAPACITY: 100 → 140.
THE PRACTITIONER HAS EXCEEDED THE SECTOR MEDIAN FOR THE FIRST TIME.】

He sat in the chair for a while afterward, lamp-lit, drenched, exhilarated in a way he intended to disclose to no one, ever, and it was in that condition — 1:52 a.m., 7%, sector median exceeded, forecast still unfinished — that Aizawa walked out of the stairwell.

She had a convenience store bag and the expression of a person who had been aware of him for longer than he had been aware of her. She did not startle. She set the bag on the nearest desk, took out two cans of coffee, set one in front of him, and sat on the edge of the desk opposite, in the lamplight, in the hum.

"Yotsuya curve," she said. "Four qi. You've been riding it out loud since Tuesday. Your breathing is visible from three meters, Mori-san, and you exhale like a man closing an account."

Kenji looked at the coffee. Then at her. The air in front of her chest held nothing he could see, but her eyes tracked something, briefly, the way you check a notification.

"You can see the ledger," he said carefully.

"No," said Aizawa. "Mine has different branding. They personalize." She cracked her can. "Mine came with a tutorial. Yours didn't, which is why you're at seven percent after four days and pleased with yourself. I was at seven percent after four *weeks*, and I had help."

Something in the way she said help made the temperature of the fourteenth floor drop a degree. Kenji looked at his forecast, at the black quadrant, at the can sweating on a coaster with the company logo, and understood that the silence had been humming this whole time not because of the air handlers.

"How many of us are there?" he asked.

Aizawa drank her coffee and looked at him over the can, and for a moment she looked tired in a way that had nothing to do with 2 a.m.

"In this building?" she said. "Sector ranking says nine. The System says I should tell you it's eight, because one of them logged off last month, permanently, at the age of fifty-one. The System sends notes about that kind of thing. You've been getting them too. Check the corner of your window, Mori-san. Small print."

Kenji Mori had never in his life ignored small print. He brought the ledger up, and there, beneath FOUNDATION ESTABLISHMENT: 7%, patient as an unpaid invoice, was a line he had been reading past for three days.

【THE PRACTITIONER IS ADVISED: KURODA, GEN. DIRECTOR, 14F. FOUNDATION: COMPLETE.】
